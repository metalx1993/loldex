about the project

One index, every LOL technique

The security community documents living-off-the-land techniques across a dozen separate catalogues — GTFOBins for Linux, LOLBAS for Windows, LOLDrivers for vulnerable drivers, WADComs and LOLAD for Active Directory. Each has its own format, taxonomy, and search box.

loldex unifies them into one normalized index you can pivot across by platform, kill-chain phase, required privilege, and capability — the query a red teamer actually runs on an engagement. Search once, across every domain, instead of tab-hopping between five sites.

Every entry carries a command, a MITRE ATT&CK mapping, the required privilege, its capability and kill-chain phase, and — where available — opsec and detection notes.

Aggregator, not re-cataloguer

loldex does not originate technique data. It normalizes and enriches what upstream projects already publish, and links back to every source on every entry. All credit for the underlying research belongs to those projects and their contributors. The data is redistributed under each source's licence.

An API, not just a site

loldex is machine-consumable. Point a SIEM, SOAR, or detection pipeline at the query API:

  • /api/search?q=certutil&os=windows
  • /api/stats
  • /api/entry/<id>

Contributing

loldex grows where it needs hands, not where upstream already has it covered. The useful contributions are:

  • Adapters for new sources (LOLESXi, LOLRMM, GTFOArgs, and beyond).
  • Active-Directory curation — AD sources are prose, and human judgement improves the mapping.
  • Opsec & detection enrichment — noise ratings, triggers, Sigma/Elastic references.

What loldex does not want is re-cataloguing of techniques the upstream projects already document — contribute there instead, and loldex will pick it up on the next sync.
